Featured Research & Engineering

Three flagship engineering projects demonstrating theoretical physics, symbolic computer algebra, and high-performance WebGL systems architecture.

Flagship 01 • Aerospace & WebGL Simulation

Orbital Command 3D

10,000+ Objects @ 60 FPS

A high-performance real-time 3D Earth tracking engine rendering and propagating over 10,000 active anthropogenic satellites and debris in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) and Geostationary Orbit (GEO).

Problem & Physics

Propagates Two-Line Element (TLE) ephemeris via analytical SGP4 perturbation models. Solves Kepler's transcendental equation ($M = E - e\sin E$) via Newton-Raphson and transforms orbital coordinates: $\text{Perifocal} \to \text{ECI (J2000)} \to \text{ECEF} \to \text{Three.js Cartesian}$.

Systems Architecture

Bypasses draw-call CPU stalls by streaming contiguous Float32Array transformation matrices into a single GPU InstancedMesh buffer. Implements sub-millisecond bounding-sphere spatial raycasting for real-time telemetry lookups.

Limitations & Edge Cases

SGP4 accuracy degrades after 48–72 hours without fresh TLE updates due to unmodeled upper-atmospheric solar flare drag. Accounts for Earth's $J_2$ oblateness, but omits high-order lunar/solar third-body gravitational harmonics.

Math Logic Engine

Calculus Rewrite Pipeline

Evaluates symbolic product, quotient, and chain rules. Implements LIATE heuristic integration by parts ($\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du$), symbolic u-substitution, and intermediate algebraic simplification passes to emit LaTeX derivation transcripts.

Flagship 03 • Computational Physics

Quantum Wave Simulation Suite

Wave-Particle Duality & Interference

Interactive computational physics simulation visualizing the Schrödinger wave equation, Young's double-slit wavepacket interference, and Born probability density distributions ($P(x) = |\psi(x)|^2$).

Wave Equation Discretization

Discretizes 2D complex wavefunctions $\psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = A e^{i(\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{r} - \omega t)}$ across an interactive field grid, calculating dynamic phase superposition and spatial wavefront propagation in sub-millisecond timesteps.

Experimental Parameters
